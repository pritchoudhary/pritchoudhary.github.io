Not every convincing animation is a reliable system

Make it work.
Then ask where it fails.

A flock can look natural and still overlap. A clear ray can leave a camera clipping through cover. The interesting lesson often starts where the simplest version stops working.

Why I’m building the lab

You set the pace

Start deliberately, pause, inspect and repeat. No background parade of ten running simulations.

The comparison stays visible

Named references and measurable outcomes let you judge the change, beyond how it looks.
