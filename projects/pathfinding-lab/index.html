Response charts, context & explanation

Graph vertices are the start, destination and visible corners of obstacles expanded by the square robot half-width. Edges connect pairs with no obstacle-interior intersection; their cost is Euclidean distance. A* uses straight-line distance as its lower bound. Dijkstra uses the same graph without the heuristic.

Boundary contact is allowed around internal obstacles; the world perimeter is excluded so a route cannot sneak around a wall flush with the warehouse boundary. This fixed-orientation square model conservatively differs from a circular or turning body. It is not a navigation mesh.
