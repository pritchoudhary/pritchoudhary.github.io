Where it helps / where to avoid it

Use for ideal artillery aiming, launcher indicators and jump landing previews. Avoid this model when drag, wind or changing gravity dominates. A landing preview also needs checks for reachable surfaces and the character’s collision shape.

One model, different constraints

A chosen launch speed leaves arrival time unknown. Gravity permits a low and a high arc when the point is reachable.

Inspect equations, reference rules and test limits

Horizontal position is vx × time. Vertical position is vy × time − ½ × gravity × time². Fixed-speed and interception constraints produce a polynomial in time. Derivative roots split it into monotone intervals; checking the boundaries also preserves the tangent solution at maximum range.

The grey reference uses 20 m/s low arc. It shares target distance, height and gravity. A missing reference is reported rather than shown as zero error. Endpoint residuals measure the ideal equation result and cannot establish a collision-free trajectory or a real gameplay hit.
