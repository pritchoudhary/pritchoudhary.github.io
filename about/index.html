ABOUT / PRITISH CHOUDHARY

I’m a Lead Unity Developer (SDE 4) based in Bangalore. I build gameplay systems, immersive learning experiences and tools that help people work with complex ideas.

I specialize in Unity development, with hands-on engineering, technical guidance, code reviews and coordination across product, design, art and QA. My work spans C# gameplay, AI, multiplayer features, VR/AR, WebGL and performance optimization. I care about how a system behaves, how a designer controls it and how we know it is working.

Unity Engineering Lab makes that approach public: small interactive studies with multiple applications, controlled comparisons and challenges you can solve yourself.

It is a place to learn through practical decisions. When does a spring help a camera? Why does a projectile miss a moving target? When is local steering the wrong tool?
